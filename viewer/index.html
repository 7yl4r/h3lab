Surface-reflectance time series on an H3 hexagon grid, built from Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 and served as static binary blocks. Two viewers, two scales: a thirteen-year site record at Rookery Bay, and a one-year regional slice across South Florida.

The caches are not published, and cannot be. The mosaic cache is 664 MB per 16-day bin — 15.3 GB for 2017 alone and ~406 GB for the full 2000–2026 record. It is a build accelerator, not the product: the archive the viewers read is 1.7 GB for the whole record, and the build regenerates the cache on demand. See docs/DESIGN.md §7.1 and decision D-29.
